## Условие

На рисунке — график $x(t)$. Определите путь тела за время от $0$ до $10$~с.

[[рис 1]]

## Рисунок (TikZ)

```latex
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=0.4cm,y=0.12cm,>=Stealth,every node/.style={font=\footnotesize}]
  \draw[xstep=2,ystep=5,brandSecondary!25,thin] (0,-10) grid (10,10);
  \draw[-{Stealth[scale=0.8]},thick,brandSecondary] (0,0)--(11,0) node[right]{$t,\ \text{с}$};
  \draw[-{Stealth[scale=0.8]},thick,brandSecondary] (0,-10)--(0,12) node[above]{$x,\ \text{м}$};
  \foreach \y in {-10,-5,5,10} \node[left] at (0,\y){$\y$};
  \node[below left] at (0,0){$0$};
  \foreach \x in {2,4,6,8,10} \node[below] at (\x,0){\x};
  \draw[ultra thick,brandPrimary] (0,10)--(10,-10);
\end{tikzpicture}
```

## Решение

$S=|{-10}-10|=20$~м.
